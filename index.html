Vertebrate Chemoreceptor Repertoire Database

Olfactory (OR, V1R, V2R, TAAR) and taste (T1R, T2R) receptor repertoires mined from vertebrate genome assemblies, combined with olfactory epithelium morphology, genome BUSCO completeness, and NCBI taxonomy.

Under construction Receptors were mined with a new chemoreceptors annotation pipeline. For now, if you use these data, please cite Policarpo et al. 2024, Nat Commun 15, 1421.

Citation

This database is under construction; receptors were re-mined with a new pipeline currently under validation. Until the corresponding paper is out, please cite:

Policarpo M, Baldwin MW, Casane D, et al. Diversity and evolution of the vertebrate chemoreceptor gene repertoire. Nature Communications 15, 1421 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45500-y

Sequences are nucleotide CDS. Downloads are grouped as functional.fa / pseudogenes.fa per genome; FASTA headers are prefixed with the receptor family.

Olfactory epithelium (OE) morphology, where shown, is compiled from the published literature and from unpublished data generated in the lab; hover the OE column for the lamellae count and the source reference.
